21CSA699A_Major Project 
Online MCA (Regular, AI) 
 
Guidelines 
 
I. Introduction 
 It is mandatory for a student who intends to pursue the major project (21CSA699A) to 
complete the registration process in the Amrita University Management System 
(AUMS), as per the academic guidelines. 
 This course consists of 18 credits. 
 The aim of a major project in the MCA program is to provide students with a 
comprehensive learning experience that combines theoretical knowledge, practical 
skills, research, innovation, teamwork, and professional development, preparing them 
for successful careers in the field of computer applications. 
 
II. Modalities 
Students have the following option to choose from the following modalities for their 
project. 
1. Individual Project 
In this mode, students enjoy the freedom to undertake an individual project, fostering 
self-directed learning and enabling a more in-depth exploration of personal interests or 
expertise. There are two options available: 
i. Independent Exploration:  
Topic Selection 
Students Should select the topic and update a shared sheet on or before the 
timeline mentioned in Section V.  
a. Students can continue exploring the project they have done for the Minor project. 
Or  
b. choose another topic from the titles shared, conduct independent 
research, and autonomously develop a project. 
Students need to fill up the form shared by the Advisor, this is to make sure 
that the topics are not same for not more than 3 students. In case the topics 
are same, the students who submitted the title last will be asked to change 
the topic.  
ii. Industry Integration: Students currently employed in a relevant industry could

III. Proposal 
 The student must submit a proposal on or before the timeline mentioned in Section 
Timeline below (format provided in Appendix A). Students doing the project as apart 
of industry integration also should share submit the proposal. 
 The student can start their project immediately unless not received any information 
regarding the change of title. 
 
 
IV. Assessments 
 Submissions 
The student pursuing a major project submits the following documents as per the 
timeline mentioned in the calendar 
1. Project Proposal 
Comprehensive overview of the research idea, methodology, expected outcomes, and 
potential impact. 
2. Interim Report 
Interim Report with the Problem statement, Background, Related work and High-Level 
Design 
3. Final Report 
The final report amends the interim report with Methodologies, Algorithms/Tools 
developed, Implementation details, future scope and conclusion 
 
 Reviews 
The student pursuing a major project undergoes a comprehensive final review via 
Microsoft Teams video call. The final review includes a presentation of the work, code 
review and demo of the application developed followed by Viva voce. 
 No supplementary examination is available for this course. 
 Continuous Evaluation marks will be finalized before the first chance 
examinations
