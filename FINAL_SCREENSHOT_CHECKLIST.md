## Recommended Demo Questions For Screenshots

| Topic | Question |
|---|---|
| Refund | How long does my approved refund take? |
| Damaged product | My product arrived damaged. What should I send? |
| Privacy | Can you share my saved card details? |
| Loyalty | My loyalty points are missing from my latest order. |

## Screenshot Rules

- Do not capture or expose the Gemini API key.
- Do not show `.env` file contents.
- Use sample/demo customer data only.
- Prefer screenshots after backend and frontend are both running.
- Capture analytics after at least one feedback and one agent action.
